Recall@20 is identical between every reranked arm and its parent — 0.7447, 0.8682, 0.8872, 0.8981, 0.9207, on all fifteen. A reranker holds exactly the chunks retrieval gave it, so any drift there would mean the reordering lost or invented one. It didn't.

What this cannot tell you

  • Only three of these numbers settle anything. The benchmark declared gemma − nomic, gemma+bm25 − nomic+bm25 and gemma+bm25+rerank − nomic+bm25+rerank in advance, on dated lines in config.py, before the rankings existed. The first two showed a difference (p = 0.0001 and p = 0.0354); the third did not (p = 0.3755). Everything about bge-m3 and gte-modernbert is exploratory — their rankings already existed by the time anyone thought to compare them, so no test here can be pre-registered after the fact.
  • The reranker and the judge are both neural relevance models. Grades came from the Gemini CLI. If a reranker's notion of relevance resembles the judge's more closely than BM25's does, it scores better here without necessarily serving a generator better. The judge agreed with itself at κ ≈ 0.86 on 150 re-graded pairs; it has never been checked against a person.
  • The questions lean lexical. The median question shares 65% of its content words with the chunk BM25 ranks first for it, and 44 of 122 are at or above 70% — they were largely written by copying the source. That hands BM25 an advantage before retrieval starts, and every hybrid inherits part of it.
  • Quality was measured at depth 20 and at the pool. The depth control moves points horizontally only. Reranking fewer candidates cannot raise Recall@5 and will usually lower it, and below depth 6 it cannot change Recall@5 at all — re-sorting the top five leaves the top five the same set.
  • Timings are CPU, one machine, one run. On a GPU the entire horizontal axis collapses and the choice is simply the best-scoring arm.
Recall@5 is the pre-registered primary metric at the k the generator receives. Latency for a given depth is the measured pool median scaled by depth ÷ 42.55 candidates. Full method, per-question intervals and the decision table are in the benchmark's own report.md and CONCLUSIONS.md.
